# Mindset for Data Engineering

Most people fail because they focus on tools instead of problems.

## What companies actually expect
- Move data reliably
- Handle failures
- Work with messy data
- Build scalable pipelines

## What beginners do wrong
- Jump to Spark without basics
- Ignore SQL depth
- No real projects

## Your goal
Become someone who can:
- Ingest data
- Transform it
- Serve it reliably

Not someone who just “knows tools”.
